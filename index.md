## About Me
I am a second-year student at University of North Carolina at Charlotte, majoring in Data Science. I have been interested in statistics on my own since 2024, and am learning both Python and R to further my analytical toolkit.

Though I have not yet worked a job in a statistical field, I worked for three years as a camp counselor at the Lake Lynn Community Center in Raleigh, North Carolina. There, I developed strong teamwork and leadership skills working with my fellow counselors.

Most of my statistical experience comes from doing research for Professor Scott de Marchi at Duke University. Under him, I investigated relationships between U.S. global military conflicts, whether nuclear weapons were involved, whether any shows of force were displayed, and several other variables. During this time, I learned a lot about multivariate analysis, and I got to work closely with both Dr. de Marchi and other aspiring data scientists. I also took AP Statistics in high school.

I am eager to earn a Master’s degree in data science, and I’m interested in pursuing a doctorate as well. Though I have not yet decided on my minor, I’m strongly considering either minoring or double majoring in mathematics with a concentration in statistics. While I am still an undergraduate student, I am focusing on both deepening my basic statistical knowledge, as well as developing the computational skills required in today’s analytical field.

For my statistical career, there are two avenues which interest me. The first is working with a team to perform rigorous statistical analysis and create incredibly effective models. My other interest is becoming a professor of statistics, both teaching undergraduate students about the field and leading research projects in fields I am interested in.
